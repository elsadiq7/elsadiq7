## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am a **Machine Learning Engineer and AI researcher** working at the intersection of **machine learning, hardware acceleration, and electronic design automation**.

My work focuses on building efficient and dependable AI systems, from model training and optimization to **FPGA deployment, RTL-aware workflows, and LLM-based engineering agents**.

I currently work at **Alpinum Systems**, where I develop agentic AI tooling for hardware verification, including automated testbench generation, failure analysis, coverage evaluation, and LLM-assisted requirements translation.

I am particularly interested in:

- ⚡ **AI hardware acceleration and hardware-software co-design**
- 🧠 **Efficient and neuromorphic AI**
- 🤖 **LLMs and agents for EDA and verification**
- 🔬 **Reliable and reproducible ML systems**
- 🖥️ **Edge AI and FPGA deployment**

I am open to **funded Research Assistant, MSc, PhD, and research collaboration opportunities** in these areas.

### 🤖 AI for EDA & Verification

Developed **AlpinumDV**, an agentic AI verification system that:

- Generates structured verification plans and UVM testbenches

### ⚡ FPGA Neural Network Accelerator

**Tech:** Verilog · Vivado · Fixed-Point Arithmetic · Xilinx KCU-105 · Python

### 🩻 FPGA Chest X-Ray Accelerator

Led the software and ML pipeline for a **15-class chest X-ray classification system**.

- Trained and evaluated MobileNetV3-Small
- Implemented **8-bit post-training quantization**
- Automated fixed-point weight export to COE/MEM
- Integrated the ML pipeline with custom RTL
- Validated FPGA outputs against the floating-point baseline

**Tech:** PyTorch · MobileNetV3 · PYNQ-Z2 · Quantization · FPGA

---

### 🧠 Spiking Neural Networks for Traffic Accident Detection

Developed six convolutional SNN architectures for video-based accident classification.

- Prepared **9,190 video clips**
- Evaluated temporal and spike-based model behavior
- Outperformed two CNN baselines
- Used **48.9% fewer parameters** than dense MoViNet models in the high-capacity configuration

**Tech:** PyTorch · snnTorch · Spiking Neural Networks · Video ML
